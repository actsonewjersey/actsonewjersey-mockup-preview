Meet ACT-SO

The ultimate youth enrichment program for high school students.

Whether you’re a science whiz or a poet, this program is your passport to take your gift to the next level and flaunt your skill on the big stage. We’ve got your back with expert mentors who will help you maximize your potential and cheer you on every step of the way.

More Details

Why join ACT-SO

ACT-SO pushes you to reach your maximum potential.

At ACT-SO meetings and workshops, students learn how to successfully compete by preparing, presenting, and doing their absolute best. Local winners qualify to compete with students from across the country at the National Competition for medals, prizes, and scholarships.

  • Learn real lessons that help you succeed both in the classroom and in life.
  • Win prizes and scholarships.
  • Build a strong college resume.
  • Have fun while developing your gifts.
  • Meet, compete, network, and make friends across the country.

General Requirements for Eligibility

High School Student

High school students enrolled in grades 9-12.

Amateur

Be of amateur status in any category of competition.

Follow Guidelines

Conform to the guideline requirements for chosen categories.

Submit Application

Submit an application to a local ACT-SO Chapter.

Submit Required Forms

Upon request by the local program chairperson and National ACT-SO Office.
