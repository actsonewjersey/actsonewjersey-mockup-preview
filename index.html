ACT-SO New Jersey

What is ACT-SO?

ACT-SO stands for Afro-Academic, Cultural, Technological and Scientific Olympics. Through workshops, mentors, and competition, students strengthen their craft, confidence, and leadership.

  • Positive self-esteem
  • Positive interaction with the adult professional community
  • Academic and artistic excellence
  • High academic and cultural achievement
