About Us

Preparing, recognizing, and rewarding excellence.

Our mission is to prepare, recognize and reward high school youth who exemplify scholastic and artistic excellence.

Our vision is to build confident youth with the character of excellence and determination to become successful productive members of our society.

National ACT-SO Brief History

An “Olympics of the Mind” that became a national youth achievement program.

1976

Vernon Jarrett presented his concept for ACT-SO to the DuSable Museum of African American History.

1977

The NAACP accepted ACT-SO as an official sponsored NAACP youth achievement program.

1978

The first National ACT-SO competition was held in Portland, Oregon with seven cities participating.

2004

Mr. Vernon Jarrett, ACT-SO founder, lost his battle with cancer at the age of 82.

New Jersey ACT-SO Brief History

New Jersey State Conference

NJ State Conference is the only NAACP State Conference that started and has maintained a statewide ACT-SO Consortium Program.

1988-1989

Rev. Sonya Redd, with the support of then President Keith Jones, developed the NJ State ACT-SO Program.

1990

NJ ACT-SO conducted its first ACT-SO Competitions with six Local NAACP Units participating as a consortium in three regions: North, Central, and South.

1991

There were 13 participating local NAACP ACT-SO Units with approximately 120 students, 24 of whom went on to compete nationally.
